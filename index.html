HOW A BUILD GOES DOWN

Work Order

  1. 01

    Send the photo

    One clear shot of your car — front three-quarter works best. DM it to us or drop it in the build sheet below.

  2. 02

    We sketch the build

    An artist hand-traces your car's actual lines, wheels and stance into a print-ready line design. Not a stock silhouette.

  3. 03

    You approve the proof

    We send a mockup on the shirt before anything gets printed. Tweak placement, colorway or text — your call.

  4. 04

    It ships

    Printed, checked, boxed. You get tracking the moment it leaves the shop.
